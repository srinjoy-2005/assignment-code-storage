Oppenheimer made significant contributions to physics in the
fields of quantum mechanics and 
nuclear physics, including the Born–Oppenheimer approximation 
for molecular wave functions; work on the theory of positrons, 
quantum electrodynamics, and quantum 
field theory; and the Oppenheimer–Phillips process 
in nuclear fusion. With his students, he also 
made major contributions to astrophysics, including the theory 
of cosmic ray showers, and the theory of neutron stars and 
black holes.
